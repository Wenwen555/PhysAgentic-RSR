# M3-R robot assets

The unmodified `franka_emika_panda/` files are from Google DeepMind MuJoCo Menagerie:

- Repository: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie
- Commit: `98a70c569c4c1b6da467a20012f766ee1f8dc521`
- Upstream folder: `franka_emika_panda`
- License: Apache-2.0; the complete upstream license is included.
- `UPSTREAM_MANIFEST.json` records every upstream URL and SHA256.

`panda_robot.xml` is a derived model generated by `scripts/build_robot_scenes.py`.
The derivation relocates the base, adds TCP/wrist sensor sites, names finger geoms,
sets common fingertip contact parameters, lowers arm servo gains and increases
gripper servo stiffness. Original arm/link inertias, kinematics, meshes, joint
limits and arm actuator torque caps are retained. The official gripper tendon
and finger coupling are retained. All mechanism variants share this one file.

The new door scenes use a lever rotating about the door-normal axis, in front of
the panel. Its mass center is on the axis (a counterbalanced lever), preserving
the old scalar handle mechanism's zero gravity torque. Handle mounting mass is
included in the door leaf's mass, so the visual mount has `mass="0"`. The handle's
separate mass/inertia are common to all robot scenes. No collision mesh is welded
to the gripper. `latch_weld` only connects the door to its fixed frame.

The old object-space MJCF files remain unchanged.
